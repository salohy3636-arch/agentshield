# AgentShield AI — Terms of Service & Service Level Agreement (Draft)

> **This is a drafting starting point, not legal advice, and it is not a finished
> contract.** Have a licensed attorney in each jurisdiction you sell into review
> and finalize this before use — especially the relief/compensation sections,
> which touch insurance and consumer-protection law. Nothing below is written to
> "get around" any licensing requirement; if your actual product design (payout
> size, marketing, who bears the risk) functions like insurance in a given
> jurisdiction, calling it a "warranty" in the contract will not change that
> legal analysis. The structure below is a genuinely conservative SaaS-warranty
> design (small caps, credits-first, human review gate on cash) specifically so
> it's more likely to sit outside insurance regulation — but that's a question
> of fact and law for your counsel to confirm per jurisdiction, not something a
> contract clause can guarantee on its own.

## 1. Nature of the Service

AgentShield AI ("Service") is a software risk-assessment and guardrail tool that
inspects, scores, and can block or hold actions proposed by a Customer's AI
agents before they execute. The Service does not provide insurance, does not
transfer risk of loss from the Customer to AgentShield, and is not a substitute
for the Customer's own risk management, insurance coverage, or human oversight
of its AI agents.

## 2. Service Credits (Primary Relief)

2.1. If the Service's audit ledger shows that a specific agent action was
     evaluated and passed (not blocked or held) by the Service, and that action
     is later shown to have directly caused a quantifiable financial loss to
     Customer, Customer may submit a claim for Service Credits.

2.2. Service Credits are non-cash, non-transferable, and applied only toward
     future subscription fees or usage on the Service. They are issued at
     AgentShield's discretion up to the per-tier cap in Customer's order form.

2.3. Service Credits are not a cash equivalent and do not constitute
     indemnification, insurance, or a guarantee of any outcome.

## 3. Optional Capped Cash Relief (Secondary, Higher Tiers Only)

3.1. On plans that include it, Customer may request cash relief in addition to
     Service Credits, subject to: (a) the per-account cap stated in the order
     form, (b) availability of funds in AgentShield's discretionary reserve,
     and (c) manual review and approval by AgentShield.

3.2. Cash relief is discretionary, is not automatic, and is not a right to
     payment upon any specific triggering event. AgentShield may decline,
     delay, or reduce any cash relief request at its discretion, including for
     reserve-availability reasons.

3.3. This section is not insurance and creates no obligation on AgentShield's
     part comparable to an insurance policy. AgentShield does not hold itself
     out as an insurer in any jurisdiction.

## 4. Eligibility / Exclusions

No relief (credit or cash) is available where:
 - The Service held or blocked the action and Customer overrode that decision; or
 - The loss arose from Customer's misconfiguration, misuse, or failure to act on
   a hold notification within the SLA response window; or
 - The loss arose from a third-party system, gateway, or data source outside the
   Service's control; or
 - Customer was in breach of these Terms at the time of the incident.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1. Except for the relief described in Sections 2–3, and to the maximum extent
     permitted by applicable law, AgentShield's total liability arising out of
     or relating to the Service, under any theory of liability, will not exceed
     the greater of (a) the fees paid by Customer in the 3 months preceding the
     claim, or (b) the applicable per-account cap in the order form.

5.2. AgentShield is not liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or
     punitive damages, or lost profits, even if advised of the possibility.

5.3. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited under
     applicable law (e.g., gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud,
     where local law prohibits capping such liability).

## 6. Customer Responsibilities

Customer remains solely responsible for: reviewing hold notifications promptly,
maintaining appropriate human oversight of its AI agents, and carrying whatever
insurance coverage it independently determines is appropriate for its business.
AgentShield recommends Customer not rely on this Service as a substitute for
such coverage.

## 7. Audit Ledger as Evidentiary Basis

All relief determinations are based on AgentShield's cryptographically hashed
audit ledger. Customer agrees this ledger is the authoritative record of what
the Service evaluated, scored, and decided for purposes of any claim under this
Agreement.

**Drafting note for founders:** the closer your cash-relief numbers get to
"meaningful money paid out based on risk of loss," the more you should treat
this as a genuine insurance-adjacent product line and loop in counsel + possibly
a licensed carrier partner (white-label/fronting arrangement) rather than
self-underwriting indefinitely. The credits-first, capped, discretionary-review
design above is meant to keep you conservative while you validate the product,
not as a permanent workaround.
